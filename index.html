Parks are sorted into 4 categories:

  • Good: nothing to worry about
  • OK: fine to run with a little care
  • Not good: think twice
  • Bad: don't run

A park takes the worst category that any of its readings reaches. Rain or showers in the forecast counts as Not good, and thundery or heavy as Bad. A park with a missing reading is rated OK at best.
